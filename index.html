What works where

Every platform renders Unicode a little differently. Use this guide as a starting point, and always paste a short test into the real field first. Limits and filters change over time, so treat the numbers on the cards as a reminder to check, not a promise.

Display names

Names appear in lists, replies and headers, which are tight spaces. A light curse reads as stylish. A heavy one can collide with the line above it and make the name unreadable.

Bios and captions

Bios have short limits, and each combining mark counts as a character. Curse a single word, keep the rest plain and leave room for the real information.

Chat messages

Chats give you more room, so heavier stacks are possible. Remember that people on phones may see clipped marks and that very long stacks can slow some apps down.

Games and usernames

Handles and game names often run through filters that reject or strip marks. If a field refuses your text, try a light level or use a style with only one mark per letter.

Make it look intentional

The best cursed names look deliberate. A cursed text generator gives you the raw material, and a few habits turn it into a good-looking result.

  • Curse one word, not the whole line. A single haunted word draws the eye, and the rest stays readable.
  • Use marks above and below in balance so the text does not lean toward one side.
  • Check dark and light themes. Marks are thin and can fade against some backgrounds.
  • Keep a plain copy of anything people may need to search for, such as your handle or a link.

Be considerate

Heavy zalgo can be hard to read with assistive technology. Use it for flavour in names and captions, and keep instructions, contact details and anything important in plain text.

Common questions

Does cursed text work on Discord?

Yes, Discord accepts Unicode in messages, nicknames and server names. Heavy stacks may be clipped in some places, so check how your name looks in the member list before you commit to it.

What is the best intensity for an Instagram or TikTok bio?

Bios are short and the fields are narrow, so low to medium intensity usually looks best. Remember that combining marks count as characters toward the limit.

Will cursed text get me banned?

Cursed text is ordinary Unicode and is not banned as such, but community rules vary. Servers and games may filter nicknames or remove text that is hard to moderate, so read the rules of the place you post in.

Why does my cursed username look normal in search?

Many platforms normalize or strip marks for search and handles. The visible display name can keep the effect while the searchable handle stays plain.

Can people copy my cursed text?

Yes. Since the effect is in the characters, anyone can select and copy it, and it will look the same wherever they paste it.

Which devices render cursed text best?

Modern phones and desktop browsers handle it well, but fonts differ. If your stack looks cut off on one device, lower the intensity and test again.
